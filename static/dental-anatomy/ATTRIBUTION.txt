Upstream processing: official 99% reduction OBJ archive converted from millimeters/Z-up to meters/Y-up and packed with signed 16-bit normals. DenTech selected 31 meshes (mandible, upper and lower gingiva, 28 permanent teeth) and concatenated their unchanged geometry bytes. Viewer applies uniform scaling, recentering, display materials, tooth condition tinting, a small lower-jaw opening, and upper-tooth root clipping above the gum line for the dental presentation. No anatomy from other datasets is included.
Source attribution: https://github.com/choxos/OMFAtlas/blob/c835665a9ade09ee0b993cee6eee1b25b7f7311b/public/ATTRIBUTION.md
This reference anatomy is not the patient's anatomy. Primary teeth and third molars are not supplied by this reference; their chart entries remain accessible by number without fabricated 3D anatomy.
