2D X-ray or report

Upload a PNG, JPEG, or PDF. Images open with zoom, pan, and rotation. PDFs open in a document viewer.

3D dental chart

Every ready document includes an illustrative model linked to the current dental chart. No CBCT is needed. Add findings in Patient > Dental chart > Update tooth, then choose which tooth conditions and notes to share.

Upload a ZIP of one complete CT/CBCT DICOM series, or a compatible multi-frame DCM file. The viewer offers 2D slices and a rotatable 3D volume.

Several ordinary X-rays do not contain enough depth information to create a true 3D scan.
